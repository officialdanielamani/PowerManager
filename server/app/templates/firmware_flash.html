Writes the firmware through the USB cable of the meter, from this browser. The meter does not need to be online, paired or even running: use it for the first flash, a changed partition layout or a meter that does not start.

  1. Plug the USB cable of the board into this computer.
  2. Press Connect and choose the port. The board is read.
  3. Choose the options and press Flash. If the board does not enter download mode by itself: hold BOOT, press and release RESET, then connect again.
The whole flash is erased: the settings, Wi-Fi and the pairing with the server are lost, and the meter has to be paired again.
